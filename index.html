THE FAMILY HISTORY

Furniture roots dating to 1917.

Desks Galore dates to 2006, while the family’s furniture history began in 1917 with a secondhand furniture store in downtown San Antonio.

The business evolved into National Furniture in 1951. The family later sold National Furniture, and has since reacquired the business. Today, Desks Galore and National Furniture continue that family furniture legacy in San Antonio.

David C. is the current owner and the great-grandson of the owner of the original 1917 furniture business.

1917 Family furniture business begins in downtown San Antonio.
1951 The business becomes National Furniture and expands.
2006 Desks Galore is started by David C.
Today New, used and coordinated furniture for homes and offices across Texas.
